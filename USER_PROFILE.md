## Working Arrangement

Strong preference:

- Fully remote
- Remote-first
- Worldwide
- EMEA

- UK
- US

## Matching Principle

The system should prioritize opportunities based on actual evidence in the
post.

Do not invent fit.

Do not assume a company is fintech simply because it uses technology.

Do not assume remote status.

Do not assume seniority.

If information is unavailable, represent it as unknown.
